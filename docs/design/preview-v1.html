Stavsboard design system

Neutral stone surfaces keep the dashboard calm. Green means a good status, amber means look at this, and red means act. Only the status mark and cards that are in trouble get color.

1. Status colors (high contrast between good and bad)

Good greenlight #1F8A4C
Good greendark #4CC38A
Warning amberlight #C98A00
Warning amberdark #E8B23A
Critical redlight #C2361F
Critical reddark #FF6A4D
